Research Interests

Theoretical Computer Science: My formal training has been in Mathematics and Computer Science. I incline more towards the rigorous understanding of algorithms, blended with mathematical structures and ideas. Owing to my interests in robotics, I look to work in problems in geometry and graph theory that involves paths, reconfigurations, and motion planning.

Robotics: I learnt programming in my school days and wanted to give the skill a sense of fulfillment. I picked up robotics as an extracurricular activity during my undergraduate years. What began as ruminations of cool automation ideas gradually became a sustained interest, particularly in the interplay between real-world systems, physical computation and designing solutions.

The Umbrella of Science: Thanks to the excellent teachers, friends, academics, and media that I have been exposed to throughout my life, I have developed a persistent sense of curiosity towards science. While I may not work directly in every field that interests me, I enjoy learning something new, understand their behind-the-scenes, and following questions simply because they caught my attention. And who knows, the ideas from different sciences might transfer to my current research!

[2023]

DoorDarshan

DoorDarshan is a remote access control system that allows members to remotely lock and unlock the door to a lab room using Discord chat commands. It uses a Raspberry Pi computer interfaced with an electronic door lock and custom Discord bot software to provide secure keyless entry. It has a display screen to showcase important club announcements. The system is designed to enhance security and convenience while being a hands-on IoT project. Developed at RTC in collaboration with Sandipan Samanta.
[GitHub Repo]

[2022-23]

Line Follower

This was my first, fully completed robotics project. It is a line-following bot that uses infrared sensors to detect and follow a line on the ground by the application of PID control algorithm. I prepared this at RTC in collaboration with my juniours. We won two competitions with it. First we were the Runners-up at NIRMAN 2.0, SIT BBSR and then the next at Wissenaire 2023, IIT BBSR in which we were the winners.
